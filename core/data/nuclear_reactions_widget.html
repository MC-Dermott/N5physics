Fusion: why it is hard to sustain

Temperature45 million K
Separation: – Relative speed: –
Both nuclei are positive, so they repel. Only very fast (very hot) nuclei get close enough for the strong force to join them. Reaching and holding these temperatures is why fusion is hard to sustain.
Temperature scale is a simplified model: the real D–T temperature is over 100 million K.
