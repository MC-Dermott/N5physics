Line Spectra: Emission and Absorption

An electron in an atom can only have certain energies. A photon is emitted or absorbed when the electron changes level, and its energy must equal the gap: E₂ − E₁ = hf.

Spectra

Emission spectrum (bright lines on a dark background)
Lines appear as electrons make downward jumps. Hover or click a line.
Absorption spectrum (dark lines on a continuous spectrum)
Dark lines appear where photons are absorbed. Absorption lines match emission lines because the same gaps are involved. A cold gas absorbs only from the ground state, so it shows fewer lines than it can emit.
