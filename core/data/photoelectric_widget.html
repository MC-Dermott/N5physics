Things to investigate
  1. Choose a metal and slowly increase the frequency from the lowest value. At what frequency do electrons first appear? What is this called?
  2. Below the threshold frequency, increase the irradiance to maximum. Are any electrons emitted? Explain using photons.
  3. Above the threshold, change the irradiance. What changes: the number of electrons per second or their maximum kinetic energy?
  4. Record Ek for five frequencies and plot the points. Find h from the gradient and W from the y-intercept.
  5. Change the metal. Does the gradient change? What changes?
  6. Raise the collector p.d. until the ammeter reads zero. Compare eV with Ek.
