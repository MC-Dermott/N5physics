Impulse–Momentum Lab

A glider slides along a frictionless air track and rebounds off the fixed buffer at one end, always travelling straight along the track. Off the buffer its momentum is constant — it only changes during the brief contact, shown as arrows: momentum arriving (blue), the force the buffer exerts while they're touching (red), and momentum leaving (green). Everything plays in slow motion, and the moment of contact is repeated, enlarged, in its own panel below so the arrows are easy to read.

Model notes. The track is treated as frictionless, so momentum changes only during contact with the buffer. Real collisions last a fraction of a millisecond; the contact time here is a model duration, deliberately stretched to hundreds of milliseconds so the force pulse has a visible shape — and the whole animation is then played back even slower than that, purely so the arrows are easy to follow. Neither slow-down changes the result: impulse still equals the change in momentum.
