Charged particle in a uniform electric field

Controls

0 = released from rest at the plate that repels it.
Measured from the right, clockwise. Within 60° of → or ← the particle goes in through the hole in a plate; otherwise it enters from the top or bottom.

Readings

Potential difference across the plates, V
Potential difference the particle has crossed
Work done on the particle by the field, W
Kinetic energy, Ek
Speed, v

Working

Plate separation 8.0 cm, plate height 6.0 cm. Edge effects are ignored. Only the electric force acts, and speeds are small enough to ignore relativity.
